**One public board, 1 winner, 384 losers. That is the point.**

Out of 385 ranked strategies, only one—Quantified Simple Monthly Rotation—beats SPY on a force-rank basis. The rest underperform. That's not a bug in the data; it's the feature. Alternative data sources (dark pool signals, consumer sentiment, liquor store traffic, electricity consumption) promise edge but deliver drag. They measure *something real*, just not what moves markets. The question isn't whether these signals are valid—it's whether validity and profitability are the same thing.
