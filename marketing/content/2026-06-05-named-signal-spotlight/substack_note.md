# What a named signal teaches us

One public board, 1 winner, 402 losers. That is the point.

Today Quantified Simple Monthly Rotation ranks #1 across 403 total algos—up 13.68% YTD while SPY sits at 11.12%. But 402 others are underwater or trailing. The gap between the top signal and the median isn't luck; it's what happens when you test thousands of ideas against real price data. Most fail. A few don't. The question isn't "why did this one win?"—it's "what does the graveyard of 402 losers tell us about signal design?"
