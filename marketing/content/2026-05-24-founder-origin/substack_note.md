# Why StockArithm exists

One public board, 1 winner, 343 losers. That is the point.

Right now, 344 trading algorithms are running live. Only one—Quantified Simple Monthly Rotation—is beating SPY. The rest are underwater or treading water. We built StockArithm because the gap between "sounds smart" and "actually works" is where most money disappears. We wanted to see it in real time, without the sales pitch. No hype. Just: here's what won today, here's what failed, here's why it matters. What would you want to know about an algorithm before you trusted it with real capital?
