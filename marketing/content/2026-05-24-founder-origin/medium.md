# Why StockArithm Exists

I built StockArithm because I got tired of pretending that most trading signals work.

For years, I watched the industry sell confidence. Backtests that looked perfect. Strategies with names that sounded scientific. Promises of alpha. Then reality would arrive—a strategy that crushed it in 2019 would crater in 2021. A signal that worked on Tuesdays wouldn't work on Wednesdays. The gap between what was promised and what actually happened was the real story, and nobody was telling it.

So I started running experiments. Not to find the one perfect algorithm. But to see what actually happens when you test hundreds of ideas against real market data, in real time, with nowhere to hide.

## The Lab, Not the Product

StockArithm isn't a trading service. It's a lab. Right now, we're running 344 signals across 105 tickers. Some are based on classical momentum research. Some are built on economic data—freight rail carloads, port container volume, retail sales. Some are pure chaos experiments, like a strategy that trades based on VIX term structure or dark pool activity.

The point isn't to find the winners and sell them to you. The point is to run them all, measure them honestly, and show you what actually works and what doesn't.

As of May 24, 2026, here's what we know:

**One signal is beating SPY.** Just one.

The Quantified Simple Monthly Rotation has returned 13.09% year-to-date, compared to SPY's 9.44%. It's been running for 112 days. It has a Sharpe ratio of 5.46 over the last 30 days. It's ranked #1 on our force leaderboard, and it's stayed there.

The other 343 signals? They're losing. Some by a little. Some by a lot.

The Chaos Rotation Lab is down 6.02% year-to-date. Copper Momentum is down 1.8%. Port Container Volume is down 2.18%. VIX Fear Rotation—which sounds like it should work in a volatile market—is down 2.35% for the year, even though it's ranked #2 over the last 30 days.

## The Honest Part

This is where most labs stop talking. They'd highlight the one winner, maybe mention a few runners-up, and sell you a subscription.

But the failures are the real data.

When you run 344 signals, you're going to find some that work by accident. You're going to find some that worked in the past but don't work now. You're going to find some that work for 30 days and then blow up. You're going to find some that look great on paper and fail in real markets.

The reason I built StockArithm is because I wanted to see all of it. Not the highlight reel. The full leaderboard. The winners and the losers, ranked side by side, updated every day.

Because that's the only way to actually learn something.

## What Changes When You See Everything

When you're forced to look at 344 signals every single day, your brain stops looking for the magic formula. You start noticing patterns instead.

You notice that the simple strategies—like monthly rotation—tend to outlast the complex ones. You notice that strategies that worked great in 2024 sometimes don't work in 2025. You notice that a strategy can be ranked #342 for the full year but #2 for the last 30 days, which tells you something about regime change.

You notice that most of the "crazy" signals—the ones built on alternative data, the ones that sound innovative—underperform the boring ones. But not all of them. VIX Fear Rotation is down 2.35% year-to-date, but it's up 6.14% in the last month. That gap matters.

You also notice that beating SPY is genuinely hard. Out of 344 signals, one is doing it. That's 0.3%. Not because the market is efficient. But because most ideas, when tested honestly, don't hold up.

## Why This Matters

I'm not building StockArithm to sell you a trading system. I'm building it because the market needs more honesty.

Every day, we publish the full leaderboard. Every signal. Every return. Every failure. No cherry-picking. No marketing spin. Just data.

If you're interested in how trading signals actually perform—not how they're supposed to perform, but how they really do—that's what we're building.

The lab is open. The losers stay visible.

And if you want to understand why one signal out of 344 is beating the market, and what that actually tells us about the other 343, that's the conversation worth having.
