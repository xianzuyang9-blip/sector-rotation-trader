# Why good signals fail in the wrong regime

One public board, 1 winner, 373 losers. That is the point.

Right now, 374 signals are running across 105 tickers. The Quantified Simple Monthly Rotation is beating SPY by 6.1% over 30 days. Everything else is underwater. Not because the other 373 are broken—they're just built for a different market. When regime shifts, the best signal from last quarter becomes noise. The question isn't which algo wins today. It's: how do you know when the regime that made it work has already left the room?
