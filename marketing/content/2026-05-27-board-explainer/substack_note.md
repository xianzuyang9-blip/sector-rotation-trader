# How to Read the Board

One public board, 1 winner, 373 losers. That is the point.

StockArithm runs two leaderboards side by side: force rank (full-window performance) and rolling 30D (last month only). They tell different stories. Force rank shows what actually worked over time. Rolling 30D shows what's working *right now*—and sometimes they diverge hard. VIX Fear Rotation ranks 372nd all-time but 3rd in the last 30 days. Chaos Rotation Lab ranks dead last overall but 14th this month. Same data, opposite conclusions. Which one matters to you?
